At Fresh Vitamins, I developed customer service, inventory management, organization, product research, and communication skills while learning to understand and meet individual needs. In my current work, I apply these experiences to AI training, data analysis, consulting, management, research, and the development of educational content and courses. My background in Humanities, Art History, and the arts also allows me to bring creativity, critical thinking, cultural analysis, and interdisciplinary perspectives to technology and data-driven work.
